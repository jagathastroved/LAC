Ayush Homa – A Sacred Vedic Ritual

Ayush Homa is a sacred Vedic fire ritual performed by erudite priests to invoke divine blessings for longevity, vitality, good health and overall well-being.

The ritual involves chanting of powerful Vedic mantras and offering oblations into the sacred fire, seeking the blessings of Lord Dhanvantari, the divine physician, and other deities associated with health and longevity.

Scriptural Reference
आयुष्मान् भवन्तु || (Rigveda 10.97.1)
Meaning: May you be blessed with long life.

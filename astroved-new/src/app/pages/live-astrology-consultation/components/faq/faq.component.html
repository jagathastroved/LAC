Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have answers to help you get started.

How long is the consultation?

Most consultations are 30 minutes. However, we also offer 60-minute sessions for in-depth guidance on complex matters. You can choose the duration while booking.

Can I reschedule my appointment?

Yes, you can reschedule your appointment up to 24 hours before the scheduled time without any additional charges by contacting our support team.

Is the consultation private and confidential?

Absolutely. We maintain strict 100% privacy and confidentiality for all our clients. Your personal details and consultation recordings are never shared with anyone.

How will the consultation happen?

You will receive a secure video/audio call link in your email and WhatsApp once the booking is confirmed. You can join the call at the scheduled time from any device.

Will I receive remedies?

Yes, based on your birth chart analysis, the astrologer will suggest practical and effective Vedic remedies, mantras, or gemstone recommendations.

What if I don't know my exact birth time?

If you do not know your exact birth time, our experts can use alternative techniques like Prashna Kundali (Horary Astrology) to answer your specific questions with high accuracy.
